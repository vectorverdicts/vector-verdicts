Apple says up to twenty percent faster CPU, and forty percent faster graphics. The camera gets a real upgrade. The main lens now has a variable aperture. Stop down to f-four for sharper group shots, or open up to f-one point four eight for low light. There's a smaller Dynamic Island, and two terabyte storage on both Pro models. Battery life is better, and fast charging hits fifty percent in fifteen minutes. So what's the verdict? It's a solid, boring update. If you're on a seventeen Pro, skip it. If you're on something older, the two-nanometer chip and the camera are worth it. The hype says revolutionary. The reality is incremental. That's fine. It's still the best phone you can buy.
